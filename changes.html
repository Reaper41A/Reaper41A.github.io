What changed

Every change to our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, newest first.

We list every change here, however small. For an important change, we also tell you in the app at least 14 days before it takes effect. The apps show the version number of the page you agreed to.

Version 2

Published 12 November 2026 · Takes effect at once for new accounts, and on 26 November 2026 for existing accounts

PrivacyTerms

Both pages

  • Now cover all three apps. Before, they only described StockFlow. Now they also cover Shopflow (shoppers) and Dropflow (delivery businesses and riders), with a section for each.
  • Everyone must be 18 or older. The old Privacy Policy said 13. The old Terms said 18. Now both say 18.
  • 14 days' notice. Important changes are now announced in the app at least 14 days before they take effect. Before, there was no set notice period.
  • Added "The short version" at the top of each page, and this What changed page.

Privacy Policy

  • Location, explained app by app. A new table says exactly when each app uses your location, why, and who sees it. This includes riders' locations while on duty and while carrying a job.
  • Who sees what on an order. A new table lists what shop staff, owners, shoppers, riders, delivery managers and our team can and can't see.
  • New things we now describe: the wallet and identity checks, delivery photos, delivery contact attempts, the "Leave it at the door" choice, searches and viewed products used to suggest shops, pantry, auto-buy and pre-buys, Shopflow Pro, the shop chat assistant, and the clipboard permission.
  • More service providers named: Google's road-distance service, OpenStreetMap and CARTO for maps, Have I Been Pwned for password checks, and Vercel and GitHub for the website and test apps.
  • How long we keep things. A new table gives the time limit for each kind of data. Before, the policy only said "while your account is active".
  • Deleting your account in the app. You can now start this from Settings › Data & privacy. Before, you could only email us.
  • Dropflow location with the app closed. While a rider is on duty, and only if they allow "all the time", location keeps going with the app closed or the screen off. A notification shows it on Android. It stops when they go off duty.
  • At the door: we now record when a rider arrives (checked against the customer's pin) and each call or text they try.
  • Added how we handle data breaches, and a section on this website.

Terms of Use

  • How held money works, step by step: paid, held, delivered, return window, paid out.
  • New: what happens when you can't be reached at delivery. The rider must arrive at your pin, try to contact you twice and wait 10 minutes. Then the order is left at the door (only if you chose that at checkout) or goes back to the shop. If it goes back, you get the price of the goods back, but not the delivery and service fees. You can report it within 48 hours if you think the rider didn't really come. If the rider was at fault, you get everything back. The delivery business is paid once the shop confirms the goods are back.
  • New: no partial refunds. A refund always covers all the goods in an order, never just some items.
  • New: return claims, written out in full: the time limit, the reasons, photos, one claim per order, and how our team decides.
  • New: a "Refunds at a glance" table showing what you get back in every situation.
  • New: delivery rules: we set delivery prices, they're fixed when you pay, and hand-over uses QR codes and photos.
  • New: rules for shops and for delivery businesses and riders, including our 10% share of delivery fees (not taken on Pro customers' trips).
  • New: Shopflow Pro. It never renews by itself.
  • Auto-buy and pre-buys. Setting them up allows us to pay those orders from your wallet. You can always skip, pause or cancel first.
  • Liability limit changed in your favour. Before: what you paid us in the last 12 months, or ₦10,000 if you paid nothing. Now: whichever of those two is larger. It also never limits what we owe you for money in your wallet or for an order.
  • Closing an account. Money that belongs to you is never kept because your account was closed.
