Do I need internet to use StockFlow?

No. You can sell, add stock and check your books with no data at all. When you’re connected, StockFlow backs everything up quietly in the background.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can start selling straight away and everything stays on your phone. Create an account when you want backup, sync across phones or staff logins.

What if I lose my phone?

If you have an account, sign in on a new phone and your shop comes back: products, sales, customers and debts.

Can my staff use it without seeing everything?

Yes. Each staff member gets their own PIN and you choose what they can see and do, for example selling without seeing your profit.

What kinds of businesses is it for?

Shops and supermarkets, wholesalers, restaurants and food vendors, phone and electronics shops, fashion stores, salons and other services, people selling on WhatsApp or Instagram, and custom-made work.

When are Shopflow and Dropflow launching?

Both are in beta now: join Shopflow or join Dropflow. Each uses its own account, separate from StockFlow.

Is my data safe?

Your records are sent over encrypted connections and only your business and the staff you invite can see them. Read our Privacy Policy for the details.
