This policy covers three apps and this website:

  • StockFlow: for shops (owners, managers and staff).
  • Shopflow: for shoppers who buy from those shops.
  • Dropflow: for delivery businesses and their riders.
  • This website, including the beta sign-up pages.

"We" and "us" means StockFlow, the team that runs all three apps. Questions? Email stockflowapp.help@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We collect only what each app needs to work. We never sell your information and never use it for adverts.
  • Each email belongs to one app only. Your StockFlow, Shopflow and Dropflow data never mix.
  • People only see what they need for an order. For example, a rider gets your phone number and exact address only after taking your job.
  • Riders' locations are shared only while they are on duty in Dropflow, and only with the shop and the customer of the job they are carrying.
  • Delivery photos are evidence. You can't delete them from the app, and only the people on that order and our team can see them.
  • You can ask to see, fix, download or delete your data at any time. Some money records must be kept by law.

1. What we collect from everyone

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google gives us your name, email address and profile picture. We never see your Google password.
  • Which app your account belongs to. We use this to stop one email being used in two apps.
  • Chats: messages, photos, videos and voice notes you send in the app, and whether they have been read.
  • Wallet: your balance, payments, money held for orders, withdrawals, and the bank details you give for withdrawals. To verify your identity we talk to you in Support chat. Your wallet records only whether you are verified, not your BVN or NIN number.
  • Support and reports: what you write to Support and any problem reports you send.
  • Device and app information: a random ID for your phone, the phone model, its system version, the app version, crash and error reports, and the screens and buttons used just before an error. We remove phone numbers, emails and passwords from these reports before they leave your phone.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, a code that lets us send them to your phone.

StockFlow2. If you use StockFlow (shops)

Without an account

You can sell without an account. Your records (products, stock, sales, customers, debts, expenses) then stay only on your phone. We only receive the crash and error reports described above.

With an account

  • Business records you enter (products, stock, prices, sales, receipts, customers, suppliers, debts, expenses, staff), so they are backed up and synced.
  • Staff: the names and emails of people you invite, and their roles.
  • Public shop profile: if you publish your shop, what you choose to show (name, category, description, address, map pin, logo, opening hours, products, prices, promos and promo videos). Anyone using the apps can see this.
  • Orders you receive and place, and their delivery records.
  • Automatic replies: if you turn on the chat assistant, it answers customers only from your shop's own catalogue, hours and delivery settings. It does not use any outside AI service.

Your customers' details

If you save a customer's name or phone number in StockFlow, you are responsible for having a good reason to keep it and for telling them. We only store it for you. Sending a receipt by SMS or WhatsApp opens your phone's own app; we don't send the message ourselves.

What shoppers' activity tells you

StockFlow shows you counts of how many people saw or opened each product. It never shows you who they were.

Shopflow3. If you use Shopflow (shoppers)

  • Delivery address and map pin, saved on your phone and attached to each order you place.
  • Orders, basket, saved items, shops you follow, pre-buys and auto-buy lists. Auto-buy and pre-buys pay from your wallet at the time you chose. You can pause or cancel them at any time.
  • My pantry: what you've bought and how fast you use it, to remind you before you run out. The reminder is created on your phone.
  • What you look at: searches, products you view, and promo videos you watch. We use these only to show you shops and products you're likely to want. Shops see counts, never your name.
  • Return claims: your reason, what you write and the photos you add.
  • Shopflow Pro: whether you are a member and until when.
  • Safe drop choice: whether you said a rider may leave your order at the door if you can't be reached.

We do not track your location in Shopflow. The map uses where your phone is only while it's open, to show shops near you. "Hot now" on the map is drawn from shops' locations, never from shoppers'.

Dropflow4. If you use Dropflow (delivery businesses and riders)

  • Business details: your company or trading name, whether you are a solo rider, your area, and what we need to verify you.
  • Riders: each rider's name, phone number, vehicle type and plate number, and whether they are on duty.
  • Location while on duty (see section 5).
  • Jobs: which jobs were offered, taken, passed on, collected and delivered, with times.
  • Proof photos: a photo of the goods at pickup and a photo at the door. Both are required.
  • Contact attempts: when a customer can't be reached, the time you arrived and each call or message you tried. This protects you if there's a dispute.
  • Earnings: fees paid to your business's wallet.

7. Phone permissions

Each app asks only when you use the feature. You can turn any of these off at any time in your phone's settings. The feature that needs it will stop working.

  • Camera: StockFlow, to scan barcodes and riders' pickup codes and take photos. Shopflow, to scan the rider's code at your door and take photos. Dropflow, for the pickup and door photos and chat photos.
  • Photos: to attach photos and save chat images when you tap Save.
  • Location: see section 5.
  • Microphone: to record voice notes and videos in chat.
  • Notifications: for orders, jobs, messages and reminders.
  • Face ID or fingerprint (StockFlow): a faster way to unlock than your PIN. This never leaves your phone.
  • Clipboard (StockFlow): Paste order asks once before reading what you copied. Change this in Settings › Data & privacy.

8. How we use your information

  • To run the apps: sync records, run shop profiles, deliver chats, place and track orders, move money, and get deliveries to the door.
  • To offer delivery jobs to the nearest riders and fix the delivery price from the road distance.
  • To show shoppers shops and products they're likely to want.
  • To send the notifications you've allowed.
  • To decide return claims and failed deliveries fairly, using the photos and records of that order.
  • To keep the apps secure, prevent fraud and fix errors.
  • To follow the law.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for adverts. We do not make decisions about you using AI.

9. Companies that help us run the apps

These companies process data only for us, to do the job listed:

  • Supabase: our database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Google: "Continue with Google" sign-in. Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications on Android. Google's road-distance service, which receives the shop's and the drop-off's map pins (not names) to price a delivery. Google Fonts for this website's text.
  • Expo: delivers notifications and app updates.
  • Paystack: processes card and bank payments. We never see or store your full card number.
  • OpenStreetMap and CARTO: draw the map pictures. Your phone asks them for the part of the map on screen.
  • Have I Been Pwned: when you set a new password, we check whether it has appeared in a known data leak. Only the first 5 characters of a scrambled version of it are sent, never the password.
  • Vercel and GitHub: host this website and test versions of the apps.

Some of these companies store data outside Nigeria. We only use providers that protect it properly. Tapping "Directions" opens Google Maps, where Google's own privacy policy applies.

We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

11. Security

Everything is sent over encrypted connections. Each shop's and delivery business's records can only be opened by that business and the people it invites. Money only moves through checks on our servers, never from the phone alone. You can lock StockFlow with a PIN.

If a data breach puts you at risk, we will tell you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

12. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask us to:

  • show you the information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • give you a copy of it to take elsewhere;
  • delete it (except records the law makes us keep);
  • stop using it for a purpose, or withdraw a permission you gave.

How: in any app, go to Settings › Data & privacy. You'll find "Delete my account" there, and links to this policy. Or email stockflowapp.help@gmail.com from the email on your account. We reply within 30 days.

Before deleting an account, empty the wallet. Money held for an open order must finish first.

If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, or the data protection authority where you live.

13. This website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you join a beta, we keep your email and which app you're testing so we can send you the test version. Our host (Vercel) keeps standard visit logs, such as your IP address and browser, to run and secure the site.

14. Age

All three apps are for people 18 or older. They handle payments and deliveries. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we'll remove it.

15. Changes to this policy

Every change is listed, in plain words, on the What changed page. For an important change, we tell you in the app at least 14 days before it takes effect. If we ever want to use your information in a new way, we will ask you first.
