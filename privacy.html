Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what the StockFlow mobile app ("StockFlow", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email stockflowapp.help@gmail.com.

Using StockFlow without an account

You can start selling without creating an account. In that case your shop's records (products, stock, sales, customers, expenses) are stored only on your phone and are not sent to us, except for anonymous crash and error reports described below.

What we collect when you create an account

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We never receive your Google password.
  • Your business records: the shop information and records you enter (such as products, stock, prices, sales, customers, suppliers, debts and expenses), so they can be backed up and synced across your devices.
  • Shop profile: if you publish your shop to the marketplace, the details you choose to show (name, category, description, address, location pin, logo, products and promotions) are visible to other users.
  • Messages and orders: chats, images, voice messages and orders you exchange with other businesses in the app.
  • Device and app information: a device identifier, app version, crash and error reports, and basic usage events, used to keep the app working and fix problems. These reports do not include the contents of your messages or passwords.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, a push token so we can send you order, message and reminder alerts.

Phone permissions

StockFlow only asks for these when you use the feature that needs them, and you can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings:

  • Camera: to scan product barcodes and take product photos.
  • Photos: to attach product and chat images, and to save chat images when you tap Save.
  • Location: to help fill in a delivery or shop address. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Microphone: to record voice messages in chat.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: as a faster way to unlock instead of your PIN. Your biometric data stays on your phone and is never sent to us.

How we use your information

  • To provide the app: back up and sync your records, run your shop profile, deliver messages and orders.
  • To send notifications you have switched on.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent misuse and fix errors.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run StockFlow. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Google: for "Continue with Google" sign-in, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications on Android.
  • Expo: to deliver push notifications.
  • Paystack: to process card or bank payments for orders. We do not store your card details.

Other StockFlow users only see what you choose to share with them (your public shop profile, and the messages and orders you send them). We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and business records while your account is active. You can delete your shop's cloud data from the app's settings, or ask us to delete your account by emailing stockflowapp.help@gmail.com. We will delete it within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep something longer.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access to each business's records is restricted to that business and the staff it invites. You can also protect the app on your phone with a PIN.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent, by emailing us. You may also contact the data protection authority in your country, such as the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

StockFlow is a business tool and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For important changes we will also tell you in the app.
