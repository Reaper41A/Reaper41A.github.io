6. Paying and the wallet

  1. You pay from your wallet or by card. Card payments are processed by Paystack under its own terms, and land in our system the same way as wallet payments.
  2. The money is held. Neither the shop nor the rider can touch it yet.
  3. The order is delivered. This is confirmed by scanning the rider's code or entering your six-digit delivery code. Then the return window starts. It is shown on your order, usually 24 hours (72 hours for electronics).
  4. The window ends with no claim. The money is paid out to the shop (for the goods), the delivery business (for the delivery fee) and us (our fees). You can release it sooner by tapping "All good".
  • A service fee is added to in-app payments. You see it before you pay. Cash and pay-later orders don't go through the app, so there's no service fee and no held money.
  • Never share your delivery code until you have your order in your hands. Sharing it confirms delivery.
  • Withdrawals go to a bank account in your own name, after you've passed an identity check.
  • Auto-buy and pre-buys (Shopflow): by setting them up, you allow us to pay those orders from your wallet at the time you chose. You get a reminder first. You can skip, pause or cancel at any time before the payment. Cancelling a pre-buy returns the money set aside.
  • If we make a mistake in your wallet, we'll fix it. If money reaches your wallet by mistake, you agree it can be taken back.

7. Delivery

Choosing delivery

At checkout you pick: pickup from the shop, the shop's own rider, or a partner rider (with a vehicle type). For partner delivery:

  • We set the price, from the road distance between the shop and your map pin. You see it before you pay.
  • The price is fixed when you pay. Detours and traffic never change it.
  • Your order is offered to the nearest available riders first. The first to accept gets it.
  • If no rider takes it, you'll see "No rider has taken it yet". You can message the shop, and the shop can cancel. If it's cancelled, you get a full refund.

Pickup and hand-over

  1. The shop scans the rider's pickup code. From then on, the order is with the rider.
  2. The rider takes a photo of the goods when leaving the shop.
  3. At your door, the rider takes a photo. Then you scan the rider's code. That confirms delivery.

If the shop uses its own rider, you give the rider your six-digit delivery code instead.

While money is held, nobody can mark an order delivered by hand. Only the scan or your code can.

Cancelling

If an order is cancelled before it's delivered, you get everything you paid back in your wallet, fees included. This includes a cancellation by the shop. The only exception is when you can't be reached at the door (next section).

8. When the customer can't be reached

This applies to partner deliveries, when the rider reaches your address and you aren't there or don't answer.

  1. The rider arrives. The app checks the rider's phone is near your map pin (within about 200 metres) before the rider can say "I've arrived". You get a notification: "Your rider is here".
  2. The rider tries to reach you at least twice, by calling or texting from the Dropflow app, at least a minute apart. Each try is recorded.
  3. The rider waits 10 minutes from arriving. The app shows you a countdown.
  4. After 10 minutes, one of two things happens:
    • If you turned on "Leave it at the door" (at checkout, or on the tracking screen before the rider arrives): the rider leaves the order at your address and takes a photo. That counts as delivered, and your return window starts. You accept the risk of leaving it there.
    • If you didn't: the rider takes the order back to the shop.

9. Problems with an order: return claims

  • You can report a problem only inside the return window, while the money is still held. This is what makes sure a refund is always possible.
  • Choose a reason: damaged, wrong item, something missing, not as described, spoiled or expired, or never received.
  • Describe what happened in at least 20 characters. Add photos (required for every reason except "never received").
  • "Never received" can't be chosen if you scanned the rider's code, or if a "Leave it at the door" photo shows the order at your address.
  • One claim per order. Filing it freezes the money until it's settled.
  • The shop answers first: it can refund you, or send the claim to our team.
  • Our team decides using the pickup, door and claim photos. The decision is one of:
    • Refund all (goods and fees), or
    • refund the goods but pay the rider (the delivery was done properly), or
    • not upheld (the money is paid out as usual).
  • You can withdraw your claim. The money is then held again, with at least one day left in the window.

No partial refunds

We don't refund part of an order, such as one item out of three. A refund always covers all the goods in the order. If only part of an order is wrong, report it, and the shop or our team decides on the whole order.
